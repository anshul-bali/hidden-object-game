Hidden Object

Seven rooms. Two worlds. One pair of eyes.
Beat the clock, find every treasure, and spot the differences.

01 / 02 Haunted A moonlit mansion of cobwebs, crypts and curses. Seven rooms hold seven sets of lost relics — the dark does not give them up easily. Enter the haunted mansion 02 / 02 Love A rose-gold manor of letters, weddings and warm light. Gather every treasure of the heart before the candles burn down. Enter the rose manor
How to play. Choose a world and explore 7 sequential rooms. Complete each room to unlock the next. Tap the named hidden treasures; the checklist tracks your finds. Rooms 5–7 show Images A and B side by side. Find a named treasure in either image to check it off in both, then find the additional differences. Rooms 5 and 6 have 5 objects + 4 differences each; room 7 has 6 objects + 5 differences. Found differences are marked with red ticks; named objects are marked with green ticks. Hint briefly highlights the next unfound object; toggle it off and on to look again. Map pauses the timer while you browse the rooms. If time runs out, you must Restart world: this reloads the world from room 1 and clears all finds. On a small screen, scroll the scene sideways to inspect the whole room. Each world has its own background music. Use the Sound button in any room to mute or unmute. Each find earns a chime. In Haunted, a sound effect marks each solved room. In Love, a guitar and piano duet celebrates each win.
